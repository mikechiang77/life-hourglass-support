# Privacy Policy

My Life Hourglass  
Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy describes the Mac app My Life Hourglass, published by Chunhua Jiang.

## What the app does

My Life Hourglass shows a quiet hourglass on your desktop for today, this year, and a life. You can enter a birth date and an age in Settings.

## Information stored on your Mac

The app keeps these items only on your Mac:

- Birth date
- The age you enter
- The hourglass position
- Whether the app opens when you log in

This information is not sent to Chunhua Jiang, to Apple, or to anyone else. The app has no account, no ads, and no analytics.

## Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or payment details. Apple handles the purchase. The developer does not receive your payment card information.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change, and the new policy will be posted on this page.
