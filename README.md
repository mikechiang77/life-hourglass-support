# My Life Hourglass

Support for the Mac app.

## What it does

A quiet hourglass sits on your desktop. Sand falls through today, this year, and a life.

## How to use it

- Double-click the glass to switch between Today, This Year, and Life.
- Left-click the hourglass icon in the menu bar to open the menu.
- Drag the glass to move it. It stays above your other windows.
- Open Settings to enter your birth date and the age you have in mind.
- You can also choose to open the app when you log in.

No account is required. Your birth date stays on your Mac.
